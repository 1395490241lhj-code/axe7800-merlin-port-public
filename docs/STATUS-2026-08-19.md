recovery, completes and packages an image. Reproduced from two independent pristine
extractions.

- **42 of 42** flat `rc/prebuild` objects → `rc` links

- `libbwdpi` / `libbwdpi_sql` built

Artifacts are **not published**. Any image is **RAW ASUS GPL BUILD ARTIFACT / UNTESTED**.

### Reproducibility

Two independent runs produced images that are **not byte-identical**. The difference is fully
accounted for by an embedded kernel build timestamp and one block of filesystem padding, with
identical FIT structural metadata and identical file counts. This is ordinary
non-reproducible-build behaviour, not a failure; no attempt was made to force determinism.

## Merlin-side blocker, restated

`priv_webapi.o` is a **Merlin-side integration requirement at the pinned baseline**. The raw GPL
package has no `libwebapi` component and does not reference it, while still building `rc` and
`httpd`.

The blocker is therefore a **cross-product-line integration boundary**, not a missing piece of
the published package.

`libwebapi` entered Merlin from the RT-AX88U / 5.02axhnd line at build 388_22525; the
RT-AXE7800 package is the 5.04axhnd.675x line at build 388_34458. The higher build number
belongs to the package that lacks the component, so this is **product-line divergence, not
generation precedence**. Build numbers are not compared across product lines to infer
chronology.

## Corrections carried forward

- An earlier extraction of the GPL package held only a small fraction of its files. Conclusions
  drawn from it — in particular that `rc` and `httpd` supply was absent — were **wrong** and
  have been corrected. The package ships both.
- Pinned-Merlin rc figures (**8/14**, **37/43**) are integration analyses of that union lineage
  and must **not** be projected backward onto GPL 388.34458.
- Earlier framing described the boundary as a *newer generation*. That is withdrawn: the
  evidence shows product-line divergence, and no chronology is inferred from build numbers
  across different product lines.
- Prebuilt-object differences derived from **set comparison** between the two trees are not
  demonstrated blockers. Six `rc` objects (`dslited.o`, `gpy211_war.o`, `ocnvcd.o`, `s46comm.o`,
  `tpvpn.o`, `v6plusd.o`) were identified that way and are **not** established as current
  blockers; most are gated by feature flags RT-AXE7800 does not set. `gpy211_war.o` remains
  **unresolved**. Only `priv_webapi.o` has been observed to stop a Merlin-side build.
